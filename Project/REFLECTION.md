# Reflection

One of the biggest things I learned in this course is that AI is only as good as the prompts you give it. Early on, when I created SAGE and the Buzzword-Larry bot, I was mostly experimenting and trying to understand how AI responds to different instructions. The outputs worked, but they were not always practical or easy to use.

My biggest “aha” moment came when I realized that small changes in prompts can have a big impact on the output. When I improved Buzzword-Larry, I only changed one rule so that the bot explains concepts clearly first before adding personality. That small change made the responses much easier to understand, while still keeping the original idea of the bot.

There were also times when AI did not give good output. For example, some responses were too wordy, confusing, or not structured in a helpful way. When that happened, I had to adjust my prompts to be more specific and clear about what I wanted. Over time, I got better at guiding the AI instead of just accepting the first answer it gave.

Another important thing I learned is that AI is better as a tool than a solution. It can help generate ideas, explain concepts, and assist with coding, but it still requires human input to refine and improve the results.

Overall, this course helped me become more confident using AI tools. I would rate my confidence now around an 9 out of 10. I feel comfortable using AI for learning, coding, and problem-solving, but I also understand its limitations and when I need to think for myself.
