# Prompt Evolution

## Early Stage (SAGE + Bad Bot)
During the early part of the course, I explored different ways of using AI. I created SAGE as a study assistant and also experimented with a personality-based bot (Buzzword-Larry). These projects focused more on understanding how AI responds to different prompts and how different styles affect output.

## Iteration Stage (Better Bot)
After testing the Buzzword-Larry bot, I realized that the corporate language made explanations harder to understand. I improved the prompt so the bot explains concepts clearly first, then adds the personality afterward. This made the responses easier to follow while still keeping the character of the bot.

## Final Stage (Project Organization)
For the final project, I organized my work to show how my understanding of prompting improved over time. This includes examples of early exploration, experimentation, and refinement. Instead of focusing on one final product, the project shows how I learned to use AI more effectively.
